Interpreting the layers

Pressure (MSLP): Low pressure frequently indicates unsettled weather. The gradient matters for surface wind.
250 hPa wind: A proxy for the upper-level jet-stream strength and position, not ground-level gusts.
500 hPa height: Helps reveal troughs and ridges in the mid-troposphere.

Model data, not measured observations. A strong jet does not guarantee a storm or a particular UK outcome. Forecast confidence must be established with ensembles and verification.

What to look for in the next development

Real meteorological contour plots (isobars and 500 hPa heights), fronts, rainfall forecasts, ECMWF/GFS model disagreement, storm-track uncertainty, and probabilistic ensemble plume maps. These require full gridded fields or a properly licensed weather-map tileset—not just this sparse sampling.
